## 🔍 Data Sources for Chart: Friction vs. Onboarding Complexity

---

🟠 Ethereum Stablecoin

- High onboarding complexity: Requires wallet + native token (ETH)
- High friction: Gas fee variability up to $80+ in congested times

Sources:

- [Ethereum Gas Tracker – Etherscan](https://etherscan.io/gastracker)
- [Consensys MetaMask Survey (2023)](https://consensys.io/blog/news/consensys-survey-reveals-attitudes-to-web3-and-crypto/)
- [Vitalik Buterin on gas cost unpredictability](https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2021/09/29/fee.html)

---

🟡 Layer‑2 Stablecoins (Arbitrum, Optimism)

- Medium onboarding complexity: Users must bridge funds and hold L2 gas (ETH)
- Moderate friction: Fees lower than L1, but require gas-token management

Sources:

- [L2Fees.info – Cost Tracker](https://l2fees.info/)
- [Arbitrum documentation](https://docs.arbitrum.io)
- [Optimism docs](https://community.optimism.io/docs/)
- [Coinbase blog on bridging](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/bridging-to-ethereum-layer-2s-arbitrum-and-optimism)

---

🟢 Solana Stablecoins (e.g., USDC on Solana)

- Moderate onboarding: Needs SOL in wallet
- Low friction: Fast tx (~400 ms), low cost (<$0.01)

Sources:

- [Solana Network Performance](https://solana.com/news/solana-network-performance-update)
- [USDC on Solana](https://www.circle.com/en/usdc-on-solana)
- [Cointelegraph: Solana Performance Metrics](https://cointelegraph.com/news/solana-network-performs-well-in-2023-data)

---

🟦 Gasless Stablecoins (Generic)

- Lower onboarding: No gas-token needed
- Some friction: Dependent on relayers; often rate-limited

Sources:

- [Meta-transactions – EIP-2771](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-2771)
- [Biconomy Documentation](https://docs.biconomy.io)
- [OpenZeppelin Defender Relayers](https://docs.openzeppelin.com/defender)
- [Gelato Relay](https://docs.gelato.network)

---

🟩 UniFi (Gasless + Low Fees)

- Low onboarding: No native token, API-driven UX
- Lowest friction: Fee abstraction, up to 84% lower onchain costs, no relayer bottlenecks

---

⚪ Traditional (UPI / Cards)

- Minimal onboarding: Built-in to banking
- Low friction: Fees are predictable (or subsidized)

Sources:

- [UPI India data](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/product-overview)
- [Visa/Mastercard fees overview](https://usa.visa.com/support/consumer/visa-rules.html)
- [NPCI annual reports](https://www.npci.org.in/)
